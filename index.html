Our mission

The BATAK Community’s mission is to foster a thriving e-sports community that promotes inclusivity, skill-building, and healthy competition.

Our values

Our community values fair play, sportsmanship, and respect for all individuals, regardless of race, gender, sexuality, or ability.

Our aim

We aim to cultivate a strong sense of camaraderie and collaboration among our members, as well as to provide opportunities for growth and recognition within the e-sports industry.
